Add bounded subject read surfaces to application declarations, immutable bundles, and the browser SDK.

Subject surfaces prove ordinary access to one parent record while exposing only AppVersion-declared relation fields, ordering, and row limits. Browser callers cannot provide child filters, fields, sorting, or limits.
